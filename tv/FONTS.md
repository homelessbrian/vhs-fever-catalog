# Fonts

All fonts in `app/src/main/res/font` come from Google Fonts and are licensed under the
SIL Open Font License 1.1: Oswald, Barlow, Barlow Condensed, IBM Plex Mono, VT323,
Playfair Display, Anton, Bangers, Creepster, Orbitron, Bowlby One.
Variable fonts were cut to single weights (Oswald 500/700, Playfair Display Italic 900, Orbitron 800).
